Adventures for tourism in Lesotho.

Explore the exciting activities that Lesotho offers to visitors.

Pony trekking

Visitors can ride Basotho ponies along mountain trails and explore remote villages and scenic areas.

Viewing ancient rock art

Discover painting made by the san people, showing people, animal and aspects of their spiritual life viewing ancient rock art.

Waterfall visits

Visit the beautiful Maletsunyane falls which drops about 192 meters and enjoy the natural scenery.

Skiing at Afriski

Exprience Skiing and snowboarding activities at Afriski Mountain Resort.

Fishing

Tsoelikana River is known for trout fishing and a neaby waterfall creats a scenic natural pool.

Cultural experiences

Visit historical sites such as Thaba Bosiu to learn about King Moshoeshoe 1 and the history of Basotho nation.
