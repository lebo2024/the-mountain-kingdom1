NATURAL BEAUTY

Katse Dam

Located in the Thaba-Tseka highlands, Katse Dam is one of Africa's tallest dams, standing about 185 metres high. Built on the Malibamatso River, it supplies water to South Africa and contributes to Lesotho's economy through royalties.

HISTORY & HERITAGE

Thaba Bosiu

Thaba Bosiu means "Mountain at Night". Located about 24 km east of Maseru, this historic flat-topped mountain became King Moshoeshoe I's stronghold in 1824 and remains an important symbol of the Basotho nation.

ADVENTURE & NATURE

Maletsunyane Falls

Near Semonkong, this spectacular 192-metre waterfall creates a cloud of mist that inspired the town's name, meaning "Place of Smoke". Visitors can enjoy hiking, pony trekking, photography, mountain biking and abseiling.

MOUNTAIN ADVENTURE

Sani Pass

Sani Pass is a spectacular mountain route connecting South Africa and Lesotho through the Drakensberg Mountains. It is famous for winding roads, dramatic views, four-wheel-drive trips, hiking, sightseeing and opportunities to experience Basotho culture.

Semonkong Lodge

This is a lodge located beside the Maletsunyane River in Lesotho, surrounded by the beautiful Maluti Mountains. It offers rustic stone-and-thatch accommodation camping facilities and a restaurant. Visitors can enjoy Mountain biking, pony trekking, hiking and adventure activities. The waterfall can be reached on foot from the lodge and also provides tourists with an oppotunity to experience Basotho culture and the natural beauty of the Maluti Mountains.
