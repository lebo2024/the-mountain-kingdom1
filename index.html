Katse Dam

Katse Dam is one of Lesotho's attractions. It is part of the Malibamatso River in the highlands of Lesotho, in the district of Thaba-Tseka. It is a double-curvature concrete arch dam about 185 metres high, making it one of the tallest dams in Africa. Its water flows through tunnels to South Africa, supplying Gauteng. Lesotho earns royalties from this.

Thaba Bosiu

Thaba Bosiu means "Mountain at Night" because legends say that it seemed to grow at night. It is a flat-topped mountain about 24 km east of Maseru and is an important historical site in Lesotho. It is the birthplace of the Basotho nation. King Moshoeshoe I made it his stronghold in 1824, after leading his people there to escape the violence of the Lifaqane, a period of upheaval and warfare across Southern Africa.

Maletsunyane Falls

Discover one of Lesotho's most spectacular Waterfalls, which is 192-meters (630ft) single drop Waterfall located near the mountain town of Semonkong in Lesotho.It stands as one of the highest waterfalls in Africa. Its immese force throws up a perpetual cloud of mist, giving the nearby town its name SEMONKONG, which translates to "The place of smoke". The best time to visit Maletsunyane is in summer when the landscapes are fresh and green.Also the falls offers many exciting activities for tourists visiting Lesotho, such as, hiking, pony trekking, sightseeing, photography and mountaing biking.Adventure lovers can enjoy experience of abseililing down the cliffs beside the waterfall.

Sani Pass

Sani Pass is a spectacular mountain route connecting South Africa to Lesotho through Drankensbery Mountain.It is famous for its winding roads, breathtaking scenery and adventuruos journeys. Tourists can enjoy four-wheel-drive trips,hiking ,mountain biking,sightseeing and photography. Guided tours also offer oppotunities to learn about Basotho culture and experience traditional village life. Sain Pass is an exciting destination for visitors seeking adventure and natural beauty in the Mountain Kingdom.
