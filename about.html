About Lesotho

Lesotho is a beautiful, mountainous country in Southern Africa. It is known as THE MOUNTAIN KINGDOM or THE KINGDOM IN THE SKY because of its high mountains,fresh air, spectacular scenery and unique culture. It is completely surrounded by South Africa, making it a landlocked kingdom.

Our Culture

Basotho culture values respect, family, community, traditional music, dancing and hospitality. Sesotho is widely spoken and traditional clothing remains an important part of cultural identity.

Our Attraction

Lesotho has many beautiful tourism attractions, including mountains, waterfall, national parks,historical sites and cultural villages which make the country a wonderful destination for people who enjoy nature,adventure and learning Basotho culture.

Discover Lesotho

Katse Dam is a remarkable engineering attraction surrounded by the beautiful mountains of Lesotho.

Thaba Bosiu is a historic mountain associated with King Moshoeshoe I and the founding of the Basotho nation.
